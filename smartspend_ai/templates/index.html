How SmartSpend estimates spending

📈
Monthly forecast
Current month's expenses ÷ days elapsed × days in month. It is a simple baseline estimate and may change as you record more transactions.
🔎
Unusual spending
Compares a transaction with the median of previous expenses. A flag is only a prompt to review, not proof of fraud.
🎯
Savings suggestions
Uses your budget, income, expenses, and largest spending category to produce basic personalized guidance.
